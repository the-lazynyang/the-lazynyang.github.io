Kettle Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026 · Kettle · Support

Kettle does not collect your data. There is no Kettle server, account, analytics or advertising. Everything Kettle uses stays on your iPhone.

What Kettle reads

When you log in to ChatGPT or Claude inside the app, Kettle uses that login to read your usage limits, reset times and plan type directly from chatgpt.com and claude.ai. These requests go only from your iPhone to those services. Your use of those services is covered by the privacy policies of OpenAI and Anthropic.

What is stored on your iPhone

  • Login session data for ChatGPT and Claude, kept in the app's web storage and the iOS Keychain.
  • Your ChatGPT account email, kept in the iOS Keychain and shown only in Settings.
  • The latest usage numbers and reset times, shared with the Kettle widget through an App Group on the device.
  • Your app settings and notification history.

Logging out in Settings removes the login data for that service. Deleting the app removes everything.

Notifications

Reset notifications are scheduled locally on your iPhone. No push notification service is used.

App settings file

Kettle downloads a small settings file from GitHub Pages (the-lazynyang.github.io) to receive notices and safety settings. It contains no personal data and Kettle sends none with the request. GitHub may record standard request data such as your IP address, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Feedback email

If you choose Send Feedback, your mail app opens a draft with the app version, iOS version, plan type and the structure of the usage response (field names and limit window lengths only, no usage numbers, email or account IDs). You can review or edit it before sending. Emails are used only to reply and improve the app.

Children

Kettle is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
