HERE TO HELP.

Have a question?

Tell us about an issue you ran into or a feature you need.

Usage shows nothing or an error.

Pull to refresh after a few minutes. Kettle waits at least 10 minutes between checks, and longer after an error, so the services are not asked too often. If it continues, log out and log in again from Settings.

I don't get reset notifications.

Check that notifications for Kettle are allowed in iOS Settings, and that a Focus or Sleep mode is not hiding them.

The numbers differ from the website.

Kettle shows the last values it received and never estimates. Check when the app last updated, and refresh if it has been a while.

A banner says updates are paused.

A service changed something on its side. Kettle stops checking until it is safe again and resumes automatically.

Is Kettle an official app?

No. Kettle is an independent app and is not affiliated with OpenAI or Anthropic. It reads usage from the same web pages you see when logged in, which may change without notice.
