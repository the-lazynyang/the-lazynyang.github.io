Common questions

Usage shows nothing or an error. Pull to refresh after a few minutes. Kettle waits at least 10 minutes between checks, and longer after an error, so the services are not asked too often. If it continues, log out and log in again from Settings.

I don't get reset notifications. Check that notifications for Kettle are allowed in iOS Settings, and that a Focus or Sleep mode is not hiding them. Notifications are scheduled on your iPhone from the last reset time Kettle received.

The numbers differ from the website. Kettle shows the last values it received and does not estimate. Check when the app last updated and refresh if it has been a while.

A banner says updates are paused. A service changed something on its side. Kettle stops checking until it is safe again and resumes automatically.

Is Kettle official? No. Kettle is an independent app and is not affiliated with OpenAI or Anthropic. It reads usage from the same web pages you see when logged in, which may change without notice.
