Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Peony keeps Health data on your iPhone and never sends Health or personal data. It downloads a small public settings file from this website for notices and the App Store link; the request contains no personal data.

1. What Peony reads

Peony requests read-only access through Apple HealthKit to Apple Watch running workouts. Workout data may include heart rate, running power, speed, stride, ground contact time, vertical oscillation, distance, steps, energy and route. Peony reads recorded values without changing, rounding or calculating them.

2. Where your data stays

Health data is read and processed on your iPhone. Peony has no server and never sends Health or personal data. The app downloads a small public settings file from the-lazynyang.github.io to show notices and the App Store link; this request contains no personal data. GitHub Pages may log standard request data such as IP address.

3. Sharing

Data leaves your device only when you choose Share or Copy and select a destination. The destination's privacy policy then applies.

4. What is stored

Peony stores your settings on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

5. This website

GitHub Pages may log standard request data. This website saves your language choice in your browser's local storage.

6. Children

Peony is not directed at children under 13.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with its updated date.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact us at the.lazynyang@gmail.com.
