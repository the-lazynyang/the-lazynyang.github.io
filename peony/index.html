PICK. EXPORT. SHARE.

Every recorded sample, in your hands.

01 / EXPORT

Choose workouts or days

Pick one or many workouts or days. Preview sample counts by metric, then share a Markdown file or copy it.

02 / RAW

As recorded, unchanged

Heart rate, power, speed, stride, ground contact, vertical oscillation, distance, steps, energy and route. Values are never changed, rounded or calculated.

03 / PRIVATE

Your data stays with you

Read-only Health access. Choose metrics and route in Settings; GPS coordinates are off by default. Nothing is sent by Peony.

Private by design. No server, account, analytics, ads or network use by the app. Data stays on your iPhone and leaves only when you share or copy a file to a destination you choose. Settings stay on device and are removed when you delete the app. Times use the time zone where the workout was recorded.

HERE TO HELP.

Have a question?

Find an answer or get in touch.

No data shows up.

Allow Peony access in Health → Profile → Apps → Peony.

A metric shows 0 samples.

The recording device did not record that metric for this workout.

The file is too large.

Choose fewer workouts or days, or turn off metrics or route in Settings.

Times look shifted.

Times use the time zone where the workout was recorded.
