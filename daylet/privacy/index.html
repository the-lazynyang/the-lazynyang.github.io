Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Daylet has no account, no server, no analytics and no ads, and the app makes no network requests. Your data stays on your iPhone.

1. What Daylet stores

Daylet stores the budget cards and expenses you enter in the app's local file on your device. Daylet does not collect this data.

2. Widgets

Your data is shared with Daylet's own widgets through an App Group on the same device. It does not leave your device.

3. Purchases

Daylet Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. Daylet does not receive your payment details.

4. Feedback email

An email is sent only if you choose to send feedback from Settings. It opens Mail with the app version, iOS version and device model in the message body, which you can see and edit before sending.

5. Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes your data from your device.

6. This website

GitHub Pages may log standard request data such as IP address. This website saves your language choice in your browser's local storage.

7. Children

Daylet is not directed at children under 13.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with its updated date.

9. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact us at the.lazynyang@gmail.com.
