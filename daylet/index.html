SET. SPEND. SEE.

One number for today.

01 / DAILY

Today's allowance

Create a budget card with a total amount and a period. Daylet adds a daily amount each day and shows what you can spend today, as Plenty left, A bit tight, Tight or Overspent. Each card stays separate, with no merging or carry-over.

02 / INSIGHT

See where it went

Add optional categories to see where you spent, and follow your spending day by day in a simple chart.

03 / WIDGETS

Home screen widgets

Check today's amount at a glance from your Home Screen. Daylet Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and unlocks multiple active cards, categories and all widgets.

Private by design. No account, server, analytics or ads, and the app makes no network requests. Your budget cards and expenses are stored on your device and shared with Daylet's own widgets on the same device. Deleting the app deletes your data.

HERE TO HELP.

Have a question?

Find an answer or get in touch.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. Daylet has no account or server, so deleting the app deletes your data.

What does Daylet Pro include?

Daylet Pro is a one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription. It unlocks multiple active budget cards, categories and all widgets.

Do multiple cards combine?

No. Each budget card stays separate, with no merging or carry-over between cards.

How do I send feedback?

Open Settings in the app and choose to send feedback. It opens Mail with your app version, iOS version and device model in the message, which you can read and edit before sending.

Is Daylet official?

Daylet is an independent app, not affiliated with Apple.
